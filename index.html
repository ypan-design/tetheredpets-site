HOW IT WORKS

We tell the story.
The rescue does the adoption.

Tethered Pet is a connector, not an adoption agency. We never take custody of an animal, never screen adopters and never collect adoption fees. Every adoption is completed directly with the shelter or rescue.

1

A rescue shares a dog

A shelter or rescue tells us which dog could use more eyes. We write the feature from their public listing, with their photos.

2

One dog at a time

We feature a single adoptable dog and follow their story over several days, so people get to know who they really are.

3

Adopt through the rescue

If you're interested, you go straight to the rescue that cares for the dog. Their process, their decision, their adoption.

WHY WE STARTED

Two rescue dogs, one idea.

Tethered Pet was started by Yifu Pan, who has adopted dogs and is now the proud owner of two rescue dogs. Shelters and rescues are full of dogs whose personalities never come through in a single photo. We want to give each of them a little more time in the spotlight, and send the people who fall for them straight to the rescue.

FOR SHELTERS & RESCUES

Let us help tell your dogs' stories.

We're looking for our first partner shelters and rescues, starting in the San Gabriel Valley and greater Los Angeles and growing from there.

  • Free for your organization. No cost, and you can stop any time.
  • Your photos stay yours. We only use what you give us permission to use.
